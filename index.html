Questions

The honest answers.

Does Tobo keep my emails?

No. Tobo reads the text of a work conversation, keeps what he understood (whose turn it is, a one-sentence summary, promises and dates, a link back to the message) and discards the text. Attachments are never opened. Your data is never used to train a model.

Who can see what?

Each teammate chooses what their own inbox shares. What is shared is visible to the whole team in the same way. There is no manager view, no ranking and no activity counter. Tobo talks about the work, never about people.

Which tools does Tobo work with?

Gmail and Google Workspace today, with Slack to ask Tobo questions. Google Calendar and Outlook are next. We finish one tool completely before starting another.

Does Tobo send emails for me?

No. Tobo never sends, edits or deletes anything. He tells you what is slipping. People decide what to do.

Why does Google show a warning when I connect?

During the private beta, Google shows an "unverified app" screen. Google's verification and an independent security assessment come before we open Tobo to everyone.

Who is behind Tobo?
