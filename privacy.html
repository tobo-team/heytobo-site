Tobo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Tobo is an AI teammate for small teams. It keeps track of what a team promised by email: who replied, who promised what, and whose turn it is. Tobo is operated by Vocaly, France. Contact: hello@heytobo.com.

This website

If you join the waitlist, we keep your email address, the team size you selected and the site you came from, to write to you about the private beta. We do not sell or share this list. Write to us to be removed. This website uses no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers.

What Tobo reads

Each member chooses to connect their own tools and can disconnect them at any time. Tobo never sends, edits or deletes anything in your tools.

  • Gmail: the headers of your work emails (sender, recipients, subject, date, message identifiers) and the text of the conversations that involve your team, so that Tobo can tell which ones are still waiting for an answer, from you or from the other side. The text is read, then discarded. Tobo never stores the body of an email, quoted replies or attachments (attachments are not opened, only their file name is seen). What Tobo keeps is what it understood: who has to move next, a one-sentence summary written in its own words, the requests and promises it found, any deadline, and the name, address, company and job title of the people outside your team. Conversations that look private (health, HR, legal disputes, personal finances) are kept as "Private conversation" with no detail. You can turn this reading off at any time in Tools: Tobo then works from headers only. Newsletters, notifications and automatic emails are ignored.
  • Google Calendar: your upcoming meetings that include someone outside the team (title, time, attendee).
  • Slack: messages in the channels where Tobo was invited, and the questions you ask Tobo.

Who sees it

Sharing is off by default. Each member decides what their own inbox shares with the team. What is shared is visible to all members of that team in the same way: there is no manager view, no ranking and no activity counter. The person who pays for the team sees nothing more than the others. Teams are fully isolated from each other. Tobo talks about the work, never judges people, and every member can see everything Tobo said about them.

How it is stored

Data is stored by Supabase (United States, AWS us-east-1). Access tokens to your tools are encrypted (AES-256-GCM). To read conversations and to answer questions, the relevant text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which does not use it to train models. This website is served by GitHub Pages. The application is closed while we move it to new hosting: this page will name the host before the private beta opens.

Deleting your data

Disconnecting a tool deletes what Tobo learned from it for you. On the "What Tobo said about me" page you can delete all your data at once. You can also write to hello@heytobo.com.

Google API Services

Tobo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or let humans read it except with your consent, for security, or as required by law.
