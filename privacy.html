Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Tobo is an AI teammate for teams: it does work in the tools a team connects and keeps one shared memory for the group. Tobo is operated by Vocaly, France. Contact: hello@heytobo.com.

This website

If you join the waitlist, we keep your email address, the team size you selected and the site you came from, to write to you about the private beta. We do not sell or share this list. Write to us to be removed. This website uses no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers. It is served by GitHub Pages and the waitlist is stored by Supabase (United States).

The Tobo app

The app is not open yet. Before the private beta opens, this page will list exactly what Tobo can read and do in each tool you connect, who can see it, where it is stored and how to delete it. The rules we already commit to:

  • What you connect stays yours. Tobo uses your access only for work you or your group asked for.
  • You choose, for every tool, what Tobo may do: blocked, ask first, or allowed. Anything that leaves the team, costs money or deletes something waits for your approval by default.
  • The group's memory is visible to its members, who can read it, correct it and erase it. Groups are isolated from each other.
  • Every action Tobo takes is logged with its steps.
  • Your data is never used to train a model. Tobo runs on Anthropic's Claude API.
  • You can disconnect a tool and delete your data at any time.

Google API Services

Tobo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or let humans read it except with your consent, for security, or as required by law.
